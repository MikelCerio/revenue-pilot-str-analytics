Case Study

From Spreadsheets to Intelligence

A real-world portfolio of 50 tourist apartments across 5 buildings in Bilbao was operating with zero centralized analytics.

🔴
THE PROBLEM
  • Revenue tracked in scattered Excel spreadsheets
  • Static pricing — same rate regardless of demand
  • No cancellation risk management or early warnings
  • No market benchmarking vs. Bilbao STR competition
  • 500+ orphan gaps per year — nights impossible to sell
🟢
THE SOLUTION
  • Unified ETL pipeline: 3 data sources → 1 clean dataset
  • ML-powered dynamic pricing based on fill speed
  • Cancellation risk scoring with LightGBM (AUC 0.82)
  • Market positioning with 1,561 Airbnb listings analyzed
  • Interactive dashboards with automated revenue alerts
